# OT Vulnerabilities and Advisories

This section covers vulnerability intelligence and secure lifecycle management for industrial assets, products, firmware, software, suppliers and supporting services.

## Master playbook

- [OT Vulnerability Management, CVE Intelligence and Industrial Product Security](OT-VULNERABILITY-MANAGEMENT-AND-CVE-INTELLIGENCE.md)

## Core principles

- Match exact product, model, firmware, version and configuration.
- Use vendor PSIRT and official government advisories as primary sources.
- Treat CVE, CWE, CVSS, EPSS and KEV as different data points.
- Prioritize using exploit evidence, reachability and process consequence.
- Use passive-first discovery and authorized testing only.
- Coordinate patches with engineering, operations and safety authorities.
- Record compensating controls and time-bound risk exceptions.
- Include SBOM, firmware provenance, PSIRT and supplier lifecycle.
- Require human approval for AI-assisted risk decisions and all operational changes.
