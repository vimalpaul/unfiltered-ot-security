# Unfiltered OT Security

**Everything OT Security. No Blind Spots.**

A global, community-driven knowledge base for operational technology (OT), industrial control systems (ICS), SCADA, industrial cybersecurity, and critical infrastructure resilience.

This project covers international standards, country and sector regulatory requirements, industrial architectures, protocols, assets, threat intelligence, vulnerabilities, defensive engineering, authorized assessments, monitoring, incident response, governance, practical labs, and AI-assisted security workflows.

## Global coverage

Coverage spans the Middle East and GCC, including Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Oman, as well as relevant jurisdictions across Asia-Pacific, Europe, Africa, North America, and Latin America. Country-specific applicability will be documented separately and supported by authoritative sources.

## Industry coverage

Oil and gas across upstream, midstream, and downstream; LNG and petrochemicals; power generation, transmission, and distribution; renewables; water and wastewater; manufacturing; mining; transportation; ports; airports; rail; building automation; and other critical infrastructure.

## Core principles

- Prefer primary sources from standards bodies, regulators, national CERTs, vendors, and peer-reviewed research.
- Record versions, dates, applicability, and review status.
- Distinguish laws and mandatory requirements from voluntary guidance and good practices.
- Map requirements to controls, assets, threats, use cases, and evidence without claiming unsupported equivalence.
- Address safety, availability, integrity, process impact, and recovery.
- Keep hands-on testing within authorized, isolated environments.
- Require human validation of AI-assisted outputs before operational or compliance decisions.
- Clearly identify assumptions, unknowns, and unverified claims.

## Documentation expectations

Substantial topics should include scope, terminology, architecture, assets and data flows, threats and operational consequences, security requirements, implementation guidance, monitoring and response, verification evidence, standards and regulatory references, practical examples, source links, version history, and review date.
