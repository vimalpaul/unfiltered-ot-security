# Project Charter

## Identity

**Project:** Unfiltered OT Security  
**Tagline:** Everything OT Security. No Blind Spots.  
**Repository:** `vimalpaul/unfiltered-ot-security`  
**Mission:** Build a global, source-backed, technically deep reference ecosystem for the security, resilience, and safe operation of operational technology and industrial control environments.

## Vision

Make high-quality OT cybersecurity knowledge easier to discover, understand, apply, and verify. Connect engineering realities with cybersecurity practice, governance, safety, threat intelligence, regulation, and research.

## Scope

The project covers OT and ICS across industrial and cyber-physical environments, including energy, oil and gas, LNG, petrochemicals, power, renewables, water, manufacturing, mining, transport, maritime, aviation, buildings, and other critical infrastructure.

Geographic coverage is global. Regional modules are organized by jurisdiction and sector, not as one undifferentiated Middle East or GCC checklist. Relevant jurisdictions include Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, other Middle Eastern markets, India and Asia-Pacific, Europe, Africa, North America, and Latin America.

## Intended audiences

- OT engineers, control system engineers, automation specialists, and plant operators
- OT security engineers, architects, assessors, penetration testers, and consultants
- SOC analysts, incident responders, threat researchers, and vulnerability managers
- CISOs, risk owners, auditors, compliance teams, and critical infrastructure leaders
- Students, educators, and researchers

## Outcomes

1. A structured OT knowledge base with practical explanations and diagrams.
2. A traceable standards and framework catalogue.
3. A jurisdiction-aware regulatory register with evidence and applicability notes.
4. Sector-specific reference architectures and use cases.
5. Defensive assessment, monitoring, incident response, and recovery guidance.
6. Isolated labs, exercises, and simulation references.
7. Human-supervised AI agent patterns for OT security work.
8. Reusable templates, checklists, glossaries, and learning paths.

## Non-goals

- Certifying an organization or declaring legal compliance.
- Reproducing licensed standards or paid content.
- Providing authorization to test real facilities.
- Publishing unverified claims as fact.
- Recommending autonomous control of safety-critical or production processes.
- Treating IT security assumptions as automatically suitable for OT.

## Quality model

Every substantial document must identify its scope, version, sources, assumptions, operational impact, safety constraints, and review status. Regulatory statements must identify the issuing authority and jurisdiction. Cross-framework mappings must explain the mapping method and limitations.

## Governance

Maintainers manage scope, review, release, and removal decisions. Technical reviewers should have relevant domain expertise. Regulatory and legal applicability statements must be conservative and clearly qualified. Safety-critical recommendations require explicit operational context and must not replace engineering authority.

## Status labels

- **Draft:** Under development; not yet reviewed.
- **In review:** Submitted for technical or source review.
- **Verified:** Sources and technical claims reviewed to the stated scope.
- **Needs update:** A source, edition, or claim may have changed.
- **Archived:** Retained for history; not current guidance.

## Disclaimer

This project is an independent educational and professional resource. It is not legal advice, engineering approval, safety certification, or a substitute for licensed standards, regulator direction, vendor instructions, or site procedures.
