## Source handling

For each new source, record exact title, publisher, edition, publication date, update date, access date, jurisdiction, source type, and claims supported. Prefer canonical official landing pages. Check whether a document is draft, final, withdrawn, superseded, or subject to amendment.

## Current research notes

- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 is the final published revision identified on the NIST publication page at the time of research.
- NIST published an initial public draft of SP 800-82 Rev. 4 on 21 September 2026 and lists 30 November 2026 as the public comment deadline. Treat it as a draft, not a final publication.
- Saudi NCA's OTCC page describes OTCC-1:2022 as minimum cybersecurity requirements for OT and as an extension to the NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls. Applicability must be checked against the official instrument and entity scope.

Last research refresh: 2026-11-17.

## Standards edition watch — 2026-11-27

- ISA's current ISA/IEC 62443 catalogue lists ANSI/ISA-62443-2-1-2024 and ISA-TR62443-2-2-2025 among published documents. Treat the series as a set of individually versioned parts, not as one edition year.
- IEC's catalogue identifies IEC 61508-1:2010 Edition 2 and a 2026 Edition 3 pre-release. Do not treat pre-release material as a final published replacement. Check each part separately.
- IEC lists IEC 61511-1:2016+A1:2017 as consolidated Edition 2.1 for process-industry SIS.
- ISO identifies ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024 as a published amendment. Keep the amendment in the controlled standards register.
- ISO lists ISO/IEC 27002:2022 as published control guidance and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as a published AI management system standard.
- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 4 is an Initial Public Draft published 21 September 2026 with comments due 30 November 2026. Rev. 3 remains the final publication until NIST issues a final Rev. 4.
- ISA's 62443 catalogue page warns against entering ISA intellectual property into AI tools or creating derivatives from ISA IP without permission. Do not upload licensed standards text into AI systems unless the applicable licence and written permissions expressly allow it.

These are metadata observations checked on 2026-11-27. They are not a substitute for checking the publisher's current lifecycle status before use.

## GCC regulatory source refresh — 2026-11-27

- UAE Government portal page for the CIIP Policy was updated 2 July 2026. Treat the policy as national CII governance context; do not infer that every OT operator is designated or that it is a detailed OT technical standard.
- DESC's official standards page states that the DESC ICS Standard applies to Dubai government and semi-government entities operating critical infrastructure and/or OT. This scope statement must not be generalized to all UAE emirates or private entities.
- Bahrain NCSC's official Baseline Cyber Security Controls page describes seven domains, including OT and IoT. The published baseline PDF states mandatory applicability for public and private entities that are part of Bahrain's CNI and encourages other entities to follow it. Confirm designation and current controlled version for each organization.
- Bahrain NCSC's published Domain 6 material includes OT/IoT segmentation, network monitoring and event logging, default-password changes, and risk-based patch testing and implementation. Apply OT change management, safety and availability constraints when translating these into site procedures.
- Bahrain NCSC's CNI page identifies sector groups and links additional sector-specific controls. Review applicable baseline and sector controls together.
- Q-CERT hosts the National ICS Security Standard v3.1. The current effective version, adoption instrument, scope and assessment process remain to be confirmed with the competent authority.

These statements are based on official authority or government pages checked on 2026-11-27. They are not a legal opinion or a determination of any specific operator's scope.

## Industry engineering source refresh — 2026-11-27

- The FBI/EPA 30 July 2026 PSA documents water-sector incidents involving internet-facing PLCs, reported operational effects including loss of pressure and flooding, and the importance of local/manual fallback.
- EPA's revised August 2024 water/wastewater cybersecurity guidance provides recommendations on asset inventory, remote access MFA, segmentation, configuration records, incident response and backup practices. These are US agency recommendations and must not be represented as universal law.
- CISA-led multi-agency asset inventory guidance includes a water/wastewater taxonomy with collection, treatment, distribution, reuse, enterprise data management, wastewater treatment and communications areas.
- NIST's 1 October 2026 water-sector OT security article discusses NIST NCCoE's water/wastewater project and SP 1800-45 secure remote access build architecture.
- NERC's CIP catalogue distinguishes currently mandatory/enforceable standards from standards subject to future enforcement. CIP-003-10 is listed for future enforcement on 1 July 2028; do not present it as already enforceable.
- NERC announced FERC approval of CIP-014-4 on 10 September 2026. Verify its effective date and implementation plan before assigning an obligation.
- TSA Pipeline-2021-01E is a historical 2025 version with a stated expiry of 2 May 2026. Do not present that version as current without verifying a subsequent directive or renewal.
