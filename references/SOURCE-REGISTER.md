## Source handling

For each new source, record exact title, publisher, edition, publication date, update date, access date, jurisdiction, source type, and claims supported. Prefer canonical official landing pages. Check whether a document is draft, final, withdrawn, superseded, or subject to amendment.

## Current research notes

- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 is the final published revision identified on the NIST publication page at the time of research.
- NIST published an initial public draft of SP 800-82 Rev. 4 on 21 September 2026 and lists 30 November 2026 as the public comment deadline. Treat it as a draft, not a final publication.
- Saudi NCA's OTCC page describes OTCC-1:2022 as minimum cybersecurity requirements for OT and as an extension to the NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls. Applicability must be checked against the official instrument and entity scope.
