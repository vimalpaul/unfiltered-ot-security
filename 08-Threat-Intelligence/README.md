## Research principles

- Start from the industrial process, assets and trust boundaries.
- Separate confirmed facts, analytic judgments and unknowns.
- Use primary sources wherever possible.
- Verify current ATT&CK for ICS technique names and identifiers.
- Do not infer attribution or process impact without evidence.
- Assess vulnerabilities using product/version, reachability, process consequence and vendor guidance.
- Keep threat hunting passive or lab-based unless active validation is explicitly authorized.
- Require human approval for AI-assisted intelligence products and operational decisions.
