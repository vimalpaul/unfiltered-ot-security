## 5. Risk analysis

Document threat scenario, exploit prerequisites, exposure, existing safeguards, likelihood rationale, safety and operational consequence, inherent risk, residual risk, and accountable risk owner. Do not rely on CVSS alone.

## 6. Evidence handling

Preserve original evidence, hashes where appropriate, collection method, timestamps, tool versions, collector identity, and chain of custody where required. Redact secrets and sensitive facility details from public artifacts.
